Before you decide

Automatic tracking requires Notification Access.

Android grants broad notification visibility. Nagi is designed to filter for supported payment notifications and process them on this device.

No bank login
Nagi does not connect to your bank account.
You can revoke access
Turn it off in Android Settings at any time. Future automatic capture stops.
Compatibility varies
Capture depends on the details your payment app includes in its notification.
What Nagi can and can’t do
Android’s access applies to notifications broadly. Nagi’s intended behavior is to filter supported payment notifications locally. The final privacy language will be verified against the production app, its SDKs, logs, backup behavior, and optional Couple Sync.
