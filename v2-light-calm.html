Nagi needs Android Notification Access.

Android's switch gives Nagi visibility into notifications from all apps. Nagi is designed to filter supported payment notifications on your phone. You can turn access off at any time.
Read what this means
Nagi does not need your bank login. Automatic capture depends on the details shown by your payment app, so some notifications may need your help. The production privacy wording will be checked against the final app, its logs, backups, SDKs, and optional Couple Sync.
